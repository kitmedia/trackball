### Major Players Analysis

#### Hudl (Market Leader - Consumer to Mid-Market)
- **Market Position**: Dominant player in amateur/semi-professional sports video (35-40% market share)
- **Key Strengths**: Market penetration, network effects, platform ecosystem, content library
- **Key Weaknesses**: Limited AI capabilities, slow processing (2-4 hours), legacy architecture
- **Pricing**: $300-$2,000/year per team depending on features

#### ChyronHego (Enterprise Leader)
- **Market Position**: Enterprise solutions for professional and elite college sports (60%+ of enterprise segment)
- **Key Strengths**: Professional credibility, advanced analytics, broadcast integration, technical excellence
- **Key Weaknesses**: High price point ($50K-$200K), complexity, long implementation times
- **Target**: Professional teams, elite NCAA Division I programs, broadcast companies

#### Veo (AI-First New Entrant)
- **Market Position**: AI-first camera + software solution (3-5% market share, growing 100%+ YoY)
- **Key Strengths**: AI-native design, hardware integration, processing speed, user experience
- **Key Weaknesses**: Hardware dependency ($4,000 camera), single camera, limited customization
- **Pricing**: $4,000 camera + $100-$200/month software

#### Coach's Eye / TechSmith (Legacy Player)
- **Market Position**: Basic video analysis for individual athletes (8-10% of consumer segment)
- **Key Strengths**: Simplicity, mobile-first, affordability, brand recognition
- **Key Weaknesses**: Limited features, no AI capabilities, single user focus
- **Pricing**: $4.99/month mobile app, $129 desktop software

#### SportsCode (Elite/Professional)
- **Market Position**: Professional analysis software for elite programs (5-8% of professional market)
- **Key Strengths**: Professional features, customization, data integration, expert users
- **Key Weaknesses**: Complexity, manual process, outdated UI, limited AI
- **Pricing**: $3K-$10K annually
